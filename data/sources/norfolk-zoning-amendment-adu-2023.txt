Amendment

An amendment to the Zoning Ordinance to modify regulations and

A. Summary of Request
This request would modify the performance standards for accessory dwelling units and
modify requirements that were put in place at the adoption of the 2018 Zoning Ordinance.
B. Plan Consistency
• The “Land Use Strategies” chapter of plaNorfolk2030 includes an outcome calling for
development regulations and practices that support a quality built and natural
environment.
o The proposed text amendments are intended to ensure that this outcome can
continue to be met, making the proposal consistent with plaNorfolk2030.
C. Zoning Analysis
• Accessory dwelling units were permitted in their current capacity with the adoption of
the 2018 Zoning Ordinance.
• An accessory dwelling unit is defined as a secondary dwelling unit established in
conjunction with and clearly subordinate to a principal dwelling unit, whether part of
the same structure as the principal dwelling unit, or as a detached structure on the
same lot, which has a separate kitchen, bathroom, and sleeping area.
• The performance standards adopted in 2018 were conservative to align with similar
uses that were permitted by state law (Temporary Health Care Structure).
o Most notably, the maximum size of accessory dwelling units and the Conditional Use
Permit (CUP) requirements have proven to be burdensome for some homeowners.
• As the amount of vacant land in the City of Norfolk has decreased, staff has seen a steady
increase of proposals for accessory dwelling units from homeowners.
• Accessory dwelling units allow homeowners a range of benefits, such as being able to age
in place or earn additional income while increasing the size of Norfolk’s deficient housing
stock. Many of these units are often more affordable than other traditional housing types.
• The proposed changes would not allow accessory dwellings units anywhere they are
currently not permitted but would provide a by-right option in zoning districts where the
use is currently only permitted with a CUP, and would provide more flexibility in general.

o Accessory dwelling units would have a by-right option in the SF-2, SF-4, SF-6, SF-10,
SF-T, R-C, MF-NS, and MF-HR zoning districts.
The proposed changes include increasing the maximum size of ADUs to 50% of the square
footage of the principal dwelling (with a cap at 800 square feet) and removing the surplus
parking requirement.
o Current regulations limit the maximum size of ADUs to 25% of the square footage
of the principal dwelling (with a cap at 500 square feet) and require one additional
off-street parking space.
Additionally, staff proposes additional changes that do not change the impact of the use
on surrounding properties.
o Removing the redundant parking arrangement.
o Removing the utility separation requirements.
o Allowing property owners to request a CUP to deviate from certain performance
standards.
Carriage houses are not included within the scope of this amendment.
o Carriage houses are unique to the Coastal Character District and are only permitted
within that character district.
o Carriage houses are a type of accessory dwelling unit but take the form of a second
story over a detached garage, which is not permitted typically. In exchange for
exceeding the normal height requirements, a carriage house must be built utilizing
higher quality materials and is subject to the Coastal Character District Pattern Book.

Ordinance No.
AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND TABLE 3.2.12 AND SECTION 4.3.3.E
OF THE NORFOLK ZONING ORDINANCE SO AS TO MODIFY AND
SIMPLIFY REGULATIONS RELATING TO ACCESSORY DWELLING
UNITS.
- - BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Norfolk:
Section 1:- That Table 3.2.12 of the Norfolk Zoning Ordinance,
entitled “Principal, Accessory, and Temporary Use Table for
Residential Base Zoning Districts,” is hereby amended so as to
allow the accessory use of “Accessory dwelling unit” in the SF-2
(Single-Family 2), SF-4 (Single-Family 4), SF-6 (Single-Family 6),
SF-10 (Single-Family 10), SF-T (Single-Family - Traditional), R-C
(Residential – Coastal), MF-NS (Multi-Family Neighborhood-Scale),
and MF-HR (Multi-Family High Rise) zoning districts either by right
or by conditional use permit, depending on and in accordance with
the performance standards set forth in Section 4.3.3.E(1)(a) of
the Norfolk Zoning Ordinance. The table shall read as set forth
in “Exhibit A,” attached hereto.
Section 2:- That Section 4.3.3.E(1)(a) of the Norfolk Zoning
Ordinance, defining and creating performance standards for the
accessory use of “Accessory dwelling unit”, is hereby amended so
as to modify and simply the performance standards by allowing the
use, by conditional use permit only, on a lot improved with a
townhouse or single-family semi-detached dwelling, on a lot that
does not meet minimum development standards, or on a lot on which
the number of off-street vehicular parking spaces are less than
the number required under the regulations of the applicable zoning
district; by increasing the area allowed for an accessory dwelling
unit constructed, by right, to the lesser of 800 square feet or
50% of the area of the principal dwelling; by removing requirements
to use the same utility connections, driveway, and mailbox as the
principal dwelling; and by removing a requirement to provide one
additional parking space more than required for the principal
dwelling.
The text shall read as set forth in “Exhibit B,”
attached hereto.
