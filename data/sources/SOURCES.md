## Norfolk Zoning Ordinance — Use Table Key

**File:** `norfolk-zoning-amendment-adu-2023.pdf` / `.txt`
**Also:** `norfolk-zoning-amendment-self-storage-2025.pdf` / `.txt`
**Source URLs:**
- https://www.norfolk.gov/DocumentCenter/View/104648/PH-01-Zoning-Text-Amendment---accessory-dwelling-units
- https://www.norfolk.gov/DocumentCenter/View/107490/11182025_PH2

**Key finding — blank cell definition:**
The following three-line key appears verbatim at the top of EVERY principal use table in the
Norfolk Zoning Ordinance (confirmed in Tables 3.2.12, 3.3.9, and 3.5.7; same format applies
to Table 3.6.11):

```
P = PERMITTED BY RIGHT
C = ALLOWED ONLY WITH APPROVAL OF A CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT
BLANK CELL = PROHIBITED
```

**Citation for briefs:**
> Norfolk Zoning Ordinance (2018), Table 3.2.12 header key: "P = PERMITTED BY RIGHT /
> C = ALLOWED ONLY WITH APPROVAL OF A CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT / BLANK CELL = PROHIBITED."
> Identical language appears in Tables 3.3.9, 3.5.7, and 3.6.11.

**Use in:** Brief 01 Section I (first statement of what blank means), and zines.

## Norfolk Planning Fee Schedule (FY2025)

**File:** `norfolk-planning-fee-schedule-fy2025.pdf` / `.txt`
**Source URL:** https://www.norfolk.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6778
**Effective:** July 1, 2024

**Key fees:**
- Conditional Use Permit (standard): **$1,080**
- Zoning Text Amendment: **$915**
- Rezoning/Conditional Rezoning: $1,375
- Certificate of Appropriateness (ARB, after-the-fact): $840
- Certificate of Appropriateness (ARB, standard): $15

**CORRECTION REQUIRED:**
All campaign materials currently cite "$5,000+" for a CUP. The actual fee is **$1,080**.
Brief 01 cites "$1,500 to $5,000." The actual range for a standard CUP is $1,080.
All documents must be updated to the verified figure.

The argument (CUP is a barrier) is not materially weakened by the correct fee — the
real barrier is the six-month timeline, two-vote process, and legal/consultant costs
on top of the application fee. Update framing accordingly.

## plaNorfolk2030 — Comprehensive Plan

**File:** `planorfolk2030-comprehensive-plan.pdf` / `.txt`
**Source URL:** https://norfolkdevelopment.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Norfolk-Comprehensive-Plan.pdf
**Note:** plaNorfolk2030 has been superseded by **NFK2050**, adopted by Norfolk City Council
in 2025. For any materials going before a public body, note that plaNorfolk2030 was the
operative plan when the campaign was drafted; NFK2050 should also be checked for consistency.

### Verified Quotes (verbatim, confirmed in extracted text)

**Outcome LU1.2** (page 2-18/2-19, "Identifying Land Use Strategies" chapter):
> "Development regulations and practices that support a quality built and natural environment."

**Action LU1.2.5** (same chapter):
> "Revise zoning regulations, developing new commercial categories and appropriate regulations
> that better reflect the current function of commercial areas as neighborhood-serving,
> community-serving, or region-serving."

**Action LU1.2.6 comment** (same chapter):
> "Design guidelines are legally required in locally designated historic districts, Pedestrian
> Commercial Overlay districts, and City and Norfolk and Redevelopment Authority (NRHA) owned
> property, including encroachments."

**Action N3.1.6** (Neighborhoods chapter) + comment:
> "Evaluate the effectiveness of Pedestrian Commercial Overlay (PCO) zoning districts, and
> consider potential ordinance revisions to ensure that they serve the purpose for which they
> were intended."
> Comment: "PCO zoning districts encourage the location of specialty retail, entertainment,
> and restaurant uses in concentrations and to a scale that encourages pedestrian movement."

**Action N5.1.2(b)** (Neighborhoods chapter):
> "Revise regulations to require that retail and commercial uses have strong ground-floor
> visual and access connections to the sidewalk, with walkability and sense of place as
> a priority."

**Action N5.1.12** (Greater Ghent area actions):
> "Continue to implement the following actions for the Greater Ghent area."
> Sub-action (d): "Utilize historic district design guidelines to ensure the development
> of appropriately designed new buildings, and promote the appropriate maintenance and
> rehabilitation of existing structures in the Ghent historic district."

**Action N5.1.20(e)-(f)** (Colonial Avenue — NOTE: refers to northern industrial section,
between railroad tracks and W. 26th Street, NOT the HC-G2 residential Ghent section):
> N5.1.20(e): "Promote the transition of Colonial Avenue between the railroad tracks and
> W. 26th Street from predominantly industrial uses to a mixture of commercial, office, and
> residential uses."
> N5.1.20(f): "Ensure that all new development and substantial redevelopment along Colonial
> Avenue between the railroad tracks and W. 26th Street provides an active streetscape by
> providing buildings set back no more than 10 feet from the right-of-way line with
> substantial first floor transparency..."

**Action N5.1.12(b):** Explore the feasibility of a Business Improvement District (BID)
for Greater Ghent. [Supports the argument that Ghent's commercial activation is a
city-endorsed goal.]
