III.

To amend the Zoning Ordinance to require a Conditional Use Permit for Self-Service
Storage Units throughout the City.
Self-Service Storage Units are permitted by-right in C-C (Community-Commercial), C-R
(Regional-Commercial), BC-O (Business Commerce-Office), BC-I (Business CommerceIndustrial), I-L (Light Industrial) and by CUP in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented Development
Support), and G-1 (Granby/Monticello Corridor Mixed Use).
This request would change the by-right designations to require a CUP.

Plan Consistency
•

plaNorfolk2030 includes a goal for
enhancing economic health and increasing employment opportunities through business
retention and expansion and business attraction.
o Outcome EV1.1 aims to foster an enhanced environment and resources to support
business expansion and investment.

o Since the proposed text amendment is intended to ensure this outcome can continue
to be met, the proposal is consistent with plaNorfolk2030.
IV.

Zoning Analysis
•

•

V.

The use first appeared in the 1992 Zoning Ordinance, which defined then as Mini
Warehouse.
o Mini Warehouse was permitted by right in BC-1 & BC-2 (Business and Commerce
Park), C-2 (Corridor Commercial), C-4 (Large Scale Commercial), I-1 (Limited
Industrial), I-2 (Light Industrial), and PD-MU 1st Bay (1st Bay Planned Development
District), and was allowed by Special Exception in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented
Development-Support) and C-3 (Retail Center).
The name of the use was changed to Self-Service Storage Units in the 2018 Zoning
Ordinance.
The Zoning Ordinance defines Self-Service Storage Units as a building or group of buildings
divided into separate self-contained units or areas of 1,000 square feet or less that are
offered for rent for self-service storage of household and personal property. The storage
units or areas are designed to allow private access by the tenant for storing and removing
personal property. Accessory uses may include leasing offices, outdoor storage of boats
and recreational vehicles, incidental sales or rental of moving supplies and equipment,
and living quarters for a resident manager or security guard. The rental of trucks or
trailers is a separate principal use and not considered accessory to this use.
Applicable performance standards for the use include:
o All doors directly accessing individual storage units shall not be located so as to be
directly visible from any public right-of-way.
o Chain-link or other types of wire fencing shall not be utilized on site .
The use is currently permitted by-right in C-C (Community-Commercial), C-R (RegionalCommercial), BC-O (Business Commerce-Office), BC-I (Business Commerce-Industrial), I-L
(Light Industrial) and by CUP in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented Development Support), and G-1
(Granby/Monticello Corridor Mixed Use).

Communication Outreach/Notification
Legal notification was placed in The Virginian-Pilot on September 11 and September 18.

VI.

Recommendation
Staff recommends that the Zoning Text Amendment request be approved.

Amend the Zoning Ordinance to require Self-Service

A. Summary of Request
• Self-Service Storage Units are permitted by-right in C-C (Community-Commercial), C-R
(Regional-Commercial), BC-O (Business Commerce-Office), BC-I (Business CommerceIndustrial), I-L (Light Industrial) and by CUP in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented Development
Support), and G-1 (Granby/Monticello Corridor Mixed Use).
• This request would change the by-right designations to require a CUP.
B. Plan Consistency
•
plaNorfolk2030 includes a goal for
enhancing economic health and increasing employment opportunities through business
retention and expansion and business attraction.
o Outcome EV1.1 aims to foster an enhanced environment and resources to support
business expansion and investment.
o Since the proposed text amendment is intended to ensure this outcome can continue
to be met, the proposal is consistent with plaNorfolk2030.
C. Analysis
• The use first appeared in the 1992 Zoning Ordinance, which defined then as Mini
Warehouse.
o Mini Warehouse was permitted by right in BC-1 & BC-2 (Business and Commerce
Park), C-2 (Corridor Commercial), C-4 (Large Scale Commercial), I-1 (Limited
Industrial), I-2 (Light Industrial), and PD-MU 1st Bay (1st Bay Planned Development
District), and was allowed by Special Exception in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented
Development-Support) and C-3 (Retail Center).

•

The name of the use was changed to Self-Service Storage Units in the 2018 Zoning
Ordinance.
The Zoning Ordinance defines Self-Service Storage Units as a building or group of buildings
divided into separate self-contained units or areas of 1,000 square feet or less that are
offered for rent for self-service storage of household and personal property. The storage
units or areas are designed to allow private access by the tenant for storing and removing
personal property. Accessory uses may include leasing offices, outdoor storage of boats
and recreational vehicles, incidental sales or rental of moving supplies and equipment,
and living quarters for a resident manager or security guard. The rental of trucks or
trailers is a separate principal use and not considered accessory to this use.
Applicable performance standards for the use include:
o All doors directly accessing individual storage units shall not be located so as to be
directly visible from any public right-of-way.
o Chain-link or other types of wire fencing shall not be utilized on site.
The use is currently permitted by-right in C-C (Community-Commercial), C-R (RegionalCommercial), BC-O (Business Commerce-Office), BC-I (Business Commerce-Industrial), I-L
(Light Industrial) and by CUP in TOD-S (Transit-Oriented Development Support), and G-1
(Granby/Monticello Corridor Mixed Use).

D. Communication Outreach/Notification
Legal notification was placed in The Virginian-Pilot on September 11 and September 18.
E. Recommendation
• Planning Staff and Economic Development have voiced concerns over the potential
impacts over the concentration and proliferation of Self-Service Storage Units throughout
the City.
• Self-Service Storage Units provide a necessary service, especially with the transient nature
of many residents due to the Navy. However, the use is inefficient on certain sites.
o The use requires large amounts of land but has low job creation and limited economic
benefit.
• The G-1 and TOD-S zoning districts both allow Self-Service Storage Units by CUP. Requiring
a CUP in all zoning districts where the use is currently allowed by-right would allow for a
site-specific analysis and discretion on sites where the use is not the most appropriate.
• Staff supports the Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment requiring a CUP for Self-Service
Storage Units in zoning districts where it is currently permitted by-right.
• The CUP process would allow for a site-specific analysis and identification of potential
benefit and low employment creation.

NORFOLK, VIRGINIA

Ordinance No. 50174
AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND TABLES 3.3.9 AND 3.5.7 OF THE
NORFOLK ZONING ORDINANCE SO AS TO PERMIT THE USE OF
“SELF-SERVICE STORAGE UNITS” BY CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT
ONLY.
- - BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Norfolk:
Section 1:- That Table 3.3.9 of the Norfolk Zoning Ordinance,
entitled “Principal, Accessory, and Temporary Use Table for
Commercial Base Zoning Districts,” is hereby amended and
reordained so as to allow “Self-service storage units” as an
principal use by conditional use permit only in C-C (Community
Commercial), C-R (Regional Commercial), BC-O (Business and
Commerce Park - Office), and BC-I (Business and Commerce Park Industrial) zoning districts. The table shall read as set forth
in “Exhibit A,” attached hereto.
Section 2:- That Table 3.5.7 of the Norfolk Zoning Ordinance,
entitled “Principal, Accessory, and Temporary Use Table for
Industrial Base Zoning Districts,” is hereby amended and
reordained so as to allow “Self-service storage units” as an
principal use by conditional use permit only in I-L (Industrial Light) zoning district.
The table shall read as set forth in
“Exhibit B,” attached hereto.
Section 3:- The Council hereby finds that this zoning
amendment is required by public necessity, convenience, general
welfare, or good zoning practice.
Section 4:- That this ordinance shall be in effect from the
date of its adoption.
